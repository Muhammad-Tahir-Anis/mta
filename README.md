## 📂 Featured Production Projects

### 1. Automated Data Pipeline & Live Analytics Dashboard (GA Stats)
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Bokeh, AWS (EC2), ETL Scripting
* **The Problem:** Internal AI modeling and manual data-labeling teams lacked real-time visibility into daily data throughput, causing bottlenecks in critical KPI reporting and operational forecasting.
* **The Solution:** Developed custom ETL scripts to pull, clean, and process raw server metrics directly from AWS EC2 instances. Built an independent microservice (**GA Stats**) that utilizes the Bokeh library to generate interactive, live-updating data visualization plots.
* **The Impact:** The system automatically refreshes every 30 minutes, keeping stakeholders synchronized and allowing management to accurately forecast project timelines based on real data trends.

### 2. Custom Shopify CRM & Workflow Digitization
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Shopify REST API, Backend Framework (Flask/Django)
* **The Problem:** A vehicle dealership suffered from severe inventory mismanagement and broken search filters on their Shopify storefront due to frequent typos and inconsistent manual tagging by staff. Additionally, customer operations (receipts, document tracking) were entirely paper-based.
* **The Solution:** Engineered a custom internal CRM seamlessly integrated with the Shopify API. Replaced error-prone text inputs with strict, standardized dropdown fields to completely eliminate tagging mistakes. Digitized their physical office operations by creating dedicated modules for customer document uploads and automated receipt generation.
* **The Impact:** Eradicated inventory data corruption on Shopify and reduced customer onboarding time by transitioning the business from paper to digital.

### 3. Enterprise Web Scraping & Catalog Automation
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Scrapy, Selenium
* **The Problem:** An aviation parts distributor relied on an incredibly inefficient process: 20 employees manually navigated multiple competitor websites every day just to track stock and update their own product catalog.
* **The Solution:** Built a cluster of 5 robust web scrapers utilizing Scrapy (for speed) and Selenium (for dynamic, JS-heavy data rendering) to target and extract product listings from complex aircraft component web portals.
* **The Impact:** Successfully automated a workflow that previously required dozens of human hours, drastically improving catalog accuracy and allowing employees to reallocate time to high-value closing tasks.
